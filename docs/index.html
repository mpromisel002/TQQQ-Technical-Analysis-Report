          Method & data

          Support detection

          1. Swing lows. A day is a swing low when its low is the lowest from 3 sessions before it to 3 sessions after it. The most recent 3 sessions can't be confirmed yet and are drawn as hollow “pending” markers.
          2. Rolling low. The lowest low of the last 3 sessions is drawn as a step line — the nearest short-term floor.
          3. Zones. Swing lows within a set distance of each other (default 0.5 × the 14-day Average True Range, about 2–3% for TQQQ) are merged into one zone. The zone price is the volume-weighted average of its lows; the band runs from its lowest to highest low.
          4. Strength. Touches 40%, recency 30%, volume at the touches 20%, bounce afterwards 10%. The table shows the top 5 zones on each side of the price.

          Resistance detection

          The same steps run on the highs: swing highs, clustered and scored the same way. A support that price closes clearly below (by more than half the cluster distance) is marked broken and becomes resistance; a resistance that price closes clearly above becomes support.

          Trend call

          Three checks: price above/below both the 50 and 200-day averages; the 50-day average's slope over 20 sessions; and whether the last two swing lows are rising or falling. All three agree → Uptrend or Downtrend; otherwise Mixed.

          Data

          Daily prices for TQQQ and QQQ, adjusted for splits and dividends, from Yahoo Finance (with Tiingo / Alpha Vantage as backups). About two years are loaded so the 200-day average is ready across the full 12-month view. A GitHub Actions job refreshes the data every weekday evening after the US close, checks for missing sessions, zero volume and suspicious jumps, and only then publishes. Current settings are saved in the page address so views can be bookmarked or shared.

          Educational use only — not investment advice. TQQQ is a 3× leveraged ETF that resets daily; it can lose value quickly, and over periods longer than a day its return can differ greatly from 3× the Nasdaq-100. Support and resistance levels are descriptions of past trading, not predictions, and levels in leveraged funds can break sharply. Data may be delayed or contain errors.
